Interactive Wood Sculpture

Design your wood wall hanging in a 3 × 3, 4 × 4, or 5 × 5 grid. Each wooden square shows a real photographed wood panel with a painted straight stripe or a right-angle corner. Select a tile, change its pattern, and rotate it in 90° steps. Drag your finger through neighboring tiles to draw a connected route: each crossed tile becomes a correctly oriented straight or corner. Triple-tap a tile to cycle through the straight, corner, and blank wood panels.

Draw with your finger: Drag from one tile through adjacent tiles. The image follows the route automatically, turning where your finger turns. To edit a single piece, use the regular tap controls. Tip: Tap an unselected tile to select it. Tap the selected tile again to rotate it clockwise. Triple-tap a tile to cycle through straight, corner, and blank designs. Your saved design stays in this browser. The wall color is saved separately. Tap the surrounding wall to try the next color.
